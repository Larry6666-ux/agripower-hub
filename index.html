The Problem

"Solar goes cheaper and cleaner." Diesel-powered processing is expensive, distant, and wasteful — and rural farmers pay the highest price of all.

Expensive

Diesel consumes 40–60% of a farmer's processing income — and every price swing erases what is left.

Distant

The nearest mechanised processors sit 20–30 km away. Fresh cassava perishes within 2–3 days, forcing distress sales.

Wasteful

Post-harvest loss destroys value farmers already grew — and women carry the heaviest burden of time and travel.

Every Agri-Power Hub is designed to:

  • Cut diesel out of processing entirely — solar-powered, off-grid capable
  • Reduce post-harvest losses by processing at walking distance, within the 2–3 day window
  • Return hours to women — 4–6 hours per batch by design, measured at pilot
  • Create decent rural jobs — young people trained and employed as hub operators
  • Anchor the community mini-grid — daytime processing load that keeps rural clean power viable
  • Keep pricing transparent — posted per-kg tariffs, every transaction recorded
